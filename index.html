Clarity

Voice navigation for the blind, NITK Surathkal

Set up
On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Clarity then opens full screen like a normal app and remembers your microphone permission.
Wake word
Say “Clarity” anytime to start hands-free
Haptic feedback
Feel a vibration pattern for turns, arrival and errors, useful with sound off
Tap to speak
Try: “Take me to SJA” · “Mess to library” · “Where am I?” · “Help”
LIVE GPS GUIDANCE
    Manual controls (for sighted helpers)
    Space speak · N next · R repeat · Esc stop
    Visual map (for sighted helpers)
